# Brazilian game classification — owner procedure

**Status (November 21, 2026):** AMI-108's bilingual `16+` screen is an **editorial advisory only**, not an official Brazilian age-rating determination. The game is available only in a protected QA environment. This release procedure is not itself an application, certificate, age-assurance implementation or legal opinion.

## Official classification paths

- The [MJSP games and apps guide](https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/seus-direitos/classificacao-1/paginas-classificacao-indicativa/jogos-e-apps) describes classification for digital games and proper use of symbols and content descriptors.
- [Portaria MJSP nº 1.048/2025, articles 43–47](https://anttlegis.antt.gov.br/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&cod_menu=7145&cod_modulo=420&nomeTitulo=codigos&numeroAto=00001048&orgao=MJSP&seqAto=000&tipo=POR&valorAno=2025) governs game/app classification. Article 45 exempts digital-only games from **prior petition** when correctly self-rated through IARC or another MJSP-approved method; Article 46 permits requesting prior analysis.
- The [IARC FAQ](https://www.globalratings.com/faq/) says its questionnaire is made available through participating digital storefronts. A developer-owned browser site should **not** assume it can obtain an IARC certificate directly from its own site.
- The [gov.br classification service](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/obter-classificacao-indicativa) offers a formal application through [CLASSIND](https://classind.mj.gov.br/) for Brazilian applicants using their own gov.br credentials.

## Shortest reliable next steps

1. **Select the actual distribution channel.** If AMI-108 will appear in an IARC-participating store, use that store's questionnaire and keep its regional rating result. If distributed only through the owner's website without an accessible authorized self-rating route, the reliable formal path is a CLASSIND request or clarification from MJSP about the authorized route for browser-only delivery.
2. **Owner action in CLASSIND, if that route applies:** sign in personally through gov.br, register a request, identify the work and applicant, and provide the required detailed synopsis and a playable copy or video demonstrating classifiable content. Include the actual Brazilian Portuguese release experience and any other materials requested by the authority; do not add private manuscripts, identity documents, passwords or credentials to GitHub.
3. **Describe the complete content honestly.** The accepted game advisory identifies violence, blood, strong language and horror; assess all actual interactive elements, and do not assume the eventual classification is 16 just because the current advisory says `16+`.
4. **Keep the result and review its display.** Retain any certificate, determination or case reference privately. Record only non-sensitive published rating, descriptors and date after confirmation. If the official age band differs from the editorial advisory, obtain owner-approved UI changes and rerun presentation checks.
5. **Only then finalize the public rating claim.** Follow the official symbol/descriptor placement instructions for the chosen distribution channel. Do not fabricate or pre-display an official classification number or icon.

## Distinct obligations and evidence

Age **classification** and age **verification/assurance** are separate questions. Do not introduce personal-data collection or biometric checks just to satisfy this document; assess any applicable digital child-protection obligations against the actual audience, distribution and account model before public launch.

No classification submission has been made here, and no official decision was verified. The owner must operate any identity-linked government application personally.
